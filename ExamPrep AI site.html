Foundation
Unit 9 • Proposal Writing 16-point assessment

Environmental Proposal

Plan first, then write a persuasive and organised proposal about an environmental challenge.

Your task

What your proposal should do
  • Make the purpose of the proposal clear.
  • Give a clear summary of the situation/results.
  • Suggest action points that can actually help with the problem.
  • Use specific Unit 9 environmental vocabulary.

Plan your proposal

1. Purpose

What environmental challenge are you addressing, and what is your proposal trying to achieve?

2. Results / situation

What has happened? What are the consequences?

3. Action points

Give practical actions. Explain how they could help.

4. Final recommendation

End by making your main recommendation clear.

Useful language

Showing results and consequences

As a result • Consequently • Due to • lead to • as a consequence

Unit 9 vocabulary

biodiversity • ecosystem • sustainable

Your Unit 9 work may also focus on topics such as plastic waste, food waste, carbon footprint, water consumption, sustainable agriculture, sustainable transport, sustainable energy, sustainable tourism, pollution, microplastics and endangered marine species.

Your proposal

Target length: roughly 140–190 words.

Before you submit
  • Is the purpose clear?
  • Have you summarised the results/situation?
  • Are your action points realistic and useful?
  • Have you used at least 2 result/consequence expressions?
  • Have you used at least 2 Unit 9 vocabulary items?
  • Is your proposal roughly 140–190 words?
  • Have you checked spelling and grammar?
